# Functional Requirements

## Project: Indian E-Commerce Business Analysis

### BR-01: Sales Performance

**Business Requirement:**
The business should be able to monitor overall sales performance over a selected time period.

**Functional Requirements:**

* **FR-01:** The dashboard shall allow users to filter sales performance by a selected date or date range.
* **FR-02:** The dashboard shall calculate and display total sales for the selected time period.
* **FR-03:** The dashboard shall display sales performance trends over the selected time period.

---

### BR-02: Sales Target Achievement

**Business Requirement:**
The business should be able to monitor sales target achievement across different product categories.

**Functional Requirements:**

* **FR-04:** The dashboard shall allow users to filter sales performance by product category.
* **FR-05:** The dashboard shall display actual sales and corresponding sales targets for each product category.
* **FR-06:** The dashboard shall calculate and display the variance between actual sales and the sales target for each category.

---

### BR-03: Geographical Performance

**Business Requirement:**
The business should be able to identify underperforming geographical regions based on sales and profitability.

**Functional Requirements:**

* **FR-07:** The dashboard shall allow users to filter sales and profitability performance by region.
* **FR-08:** The dashboard shall identify regions with comparatively low sales and profitability performance.
* **FR-09:** The dashboard shall allow users to drill down from state-level performance to city-level performance.

---

### BR-04: Profitability Performance

**Business Requirement:**
The business should be able to monitor profitability across different regions.

**Functional Requirements:**

* **FR-10:** The dashboard shall allow users to filter profitability performance by region.
* **FR-11:** The dashboard shall calculate and display total profit for each region.
* **FR-12:** The dashboard shall display profit by category and sub-category to identify profitable and loss-making areas.

---

### BR-05: Category and Sub-Category Performance

**Business Requirement:**
The business should be able to evaluate the performance of different product categories and sub-categories.

**Functional Requirements:**

* **FR-13:** The dashboard shall allow users to filter performance by category and sub-category.
* **FR-14:** The dashboard shall display sales, profit, and quantity performance by category and sub-category.
* **FR-15:** The dashboard shall allow users to compare the performance of different categories and sub-categories based on sales and profit.
