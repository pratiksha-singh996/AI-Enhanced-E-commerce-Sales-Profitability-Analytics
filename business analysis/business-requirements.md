**BR-01:** The business should be able to monitor overall sales performance over a selected time period.

**BR-02:** The business should be able to monitor sales target achievement across different product categories.

**BR-03:** The business should be able to identify underperforming geographical regions based on sales and profitability.

**BR-04:** The business should be able to monitor profitability across different regions.

**BR-05:** The business should be able to evaluate the performance of different product categories and sub-categories.
